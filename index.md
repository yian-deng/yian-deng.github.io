## About Me

I am a Senior Research Engineer at the Beijing Institute of General Artificial Intelligence (BIGAI), where I have been working on the development of general-purpose intelligent agents since 2024. My current work focuses on agent cognitive architectures, planning and decision-making, and continual learning, with applications in TongAI, TongWorld, and embodied robotic systems.

I design and develop agent mechanisms that connect perception, cognition, planning, execution, feedback, reflection, and skill acquisition into a closed learning loop. My work includes asynchronous planning, parallel action execution, expectation-based action cancellation, feedback-driven task reflection, and efficient reuse of historical reasoning processes. I also work on the continuous improvement of general-purpose agent models through data collection, supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF).

Before joining BIGAI, I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Manchester (2024), and my M.S. and B.S. degrees from Peking University and Harbin Institute of Technology, respectively. My earlier research covered Riemannian optimization, adversarial robustness, deep reinforcement learning, and robot learning. I also gained industrial R&D experience at Baidu, Microsoft Research Asia, and Schlumberger, working on autonomous driving perception, OCR, and reinforcement learning-based control.

My research interests lie at the intersection of agent intelligence, cognitive planning, continual learning, embodied intelligence, and reinforcement learning, with a long-term goal of building intelligent agents capable of autonomous reasoning, adaptation, and skill development.

## Research Interests

- **Agent Intelligence**: Cognitive architectures, agent planning, asynchronous decision-making, and recursive self-improvement
- **Continual Agent Learning**: Feedback-driven reflection, skill acquisition, self-improvement, and reinforcement learning
- **Embodied Intelligence**: Embodied agents, robotic learning, action execution, and multimodal perception
- **Machine Learning Foundations**: Optimization algorithms, adversarial robustness, and deep reinforcement learning
