## About Me

I am a Senior Research Engineer at the Beijing Institute of General Artificial Intelligence (BIGAI), where I have been working on general-purpose intelligent agents since 2024. My research focuses on the design of cognitive architectures for intelligent agents, planning and decision-making, and continual learning, with applications in TongAI, TongWorld, and embodied robotic systems. My long-term goal is to develop intelligent agents capable of autonomous reasoning, learning, and adaptation in dynamic environments.

Before joining BIGAI, I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Manchester (2024, supervised by Tingting Mu), and my M.S. and B.S. degrees from Peking University (supervised by Dingsheng Luo) and Harbin Institute of Technology, respectively. My earlier research covered Riemannian optimization, adversarial robustness, deep reinforcement learning, and robot learning. I also gained industrial R&D experience at Baidu, Microsoft Research Asia, and Schlumberger, working on autonomous driving perception, OCR, and reinforcement learning-based control.

## Research Interests

- **Agent Intelligence**: Cognitive architectures, agent planning, asynchronous decision-making, and recursive self-improvement
- **Continual Agent Learning**: Feedback-driven reflection, skill acquisition, self-improvement, and reinforcement learning
- **Embodied Intelligence**: Embodied agents, robotic learning, action execution, and multimodal perception
- **Machine Learning Foundations**: Optimization algorithms, adversarial robustness, and deep reinforcement learning
